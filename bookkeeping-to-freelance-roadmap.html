FAQ

Before you start.

A few straightforward answers.

Do I need to buy accounting software?
No. The roadmap is built around free trials of QuickBooks and Xero, or Wave Accounting, which is free permanently. You don't need to commit to a paid accounting software subscription just to work through the workbook.
Is this a video course?
No. It's an interactive checklist-and-quiz workbook. You'll work through concrete tasks, check your understanding, and use curated free external resources when they're useful. The goal is active progress rather than passive watching.
How long does this take?
Realistically, several weeks of learning, practice, and profile-building before your first paid client. Your timeline will depend on your starting point, available time, quality of your practice, and the freelance market you're applying to. The roadmap is intentionally upfront about this rather than making get-rich-quick claims.
Can I use this on mobile?
Yes. The workbook is responsive and designed to work in any modern mobile browser as well as on desktop.
Do I need a degree or bookkeeping experience?
No degree or previous bookkeeping experience is required to start the roadmap. It begins with the fundamentals and moves progressively toward hands-on practice, portfolio building, applications, and client delivery.
Does the workbook guarantee that I'll get a client?
No — and it shouldn't. Freelance work depends on your skills, effort, communication, market conditions, applications, and many factors outside the workbook. What it gives you is a structured process for becoming prepared and taking sensible steps toward your first client.
